Privacy Policy

Last updated — draft, not yet published

Draft for legal review. This page outlines the intended shape of the policy based on the app's actual data handling (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Gemini API). It has not been reviewed by counsel and should not be treated as a final, binding policy until it has.

What we collect

De{c0}dE collects the information needed to operate your account and the code you choose to submit for analysis:

  • Account information from Firebase Authentication (email, or your Google account's basic profile if you sign in with Google).
  • Code snippets you paste for analysis, and the resulting AI analysis, conversations, and follow-up questions — stored in Cloud Firestore under your account.
  • Basic app preferences (theme, workspace density, explanation level).

How it's used

Your code and questions are sent to the Gemini API to generate the analysis you see. Your data is used to operate your workspace, save your history, and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Account and workspace data is stored in Cloud Firestore under your user ID, protected by Firebase Security Rules so only you can read or write your own data.

Your controls

You can edit your profile, change preferences, and permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Settings page at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@aymanissa.dev.
